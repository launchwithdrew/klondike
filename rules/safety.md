# Safety — what never posts

New accounts get struck fast, and one strike costs more than ten clips earn. When in doubt, skip.

**The engine refuses or flags automatically:** sexual content (said, in viewer-clip titles, in chat, or
in your headline — censoring words does not make a clip safe) · slurs and profanity (bleeped, and the
bleep is checked to be silent) · copyright-muted audio · violence wording in headlines · polls where
chat obeys instead of reacting · repeated lines (song lyrics, text-to-speech, loops) · the game's own
cutscenes, menus and loading screens.

**Only you catch these — look at the frames:** nudity, body close-ups, zoomed shots, strip clubs,
gambling, a song playing as the clip (copyright claim).

**Headlines:** true to what's on screen; never invent a number, name or event.

`--frames-checked` means you looked at the frames and read the captions. Never pass it otherwise.
